2. EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
==============================================================================
  Strains (9)      : DV10, GALA, OPA, SAI, TXL, VIN13, W12, X16, X5
  Temperature (4)  : 12, 18, 25, TVAR   (TVAR = time-varying regime)
  Initial sugar (2): 210, 250 g/L  (NOMINAL label - see data-quality note)
  Replicates (3)   : R1, R2, R3  (biological)
  Sampling points  : t0..t8 (9 per fermentation), 105 distinct clock times

  => 9 strains x 4 temperatures x 2 sugar levels x 3 reps = 216 fermentation
     units; x 9 sampling points = 1944 observations. FULLY BALANCED.
     The raw sheet has 1950 rows: 6 extra 't5bis' samples taken only for
     TXL and VIN13 at 18 degC / 250 g/L. Excluded to keep the design balanced.

  Design type: three-way factorial (Strain x Temperature x InitialSugar)
     with REPEATED MEASURES over 9 sampling points on each unit.
     => observations within a unit are NOT independent; any test must either
        collapse each unit to one summary value, or model the repetition.

  t0..t8 are *nominal* labels, not fixed clock times. The mean time to the
  final sample is 725 h at 12 degC but 284 h at 25 degC, and it varies
  between strains within one temperature (at 12 degC: W12 671 h vs
  VIN13 803 h). Sampling was event-driven - samples were taken as each
  fermentation progressed.
  CONSEQUENCES:
   - never average a variable across strains at a given t-label as if the
     elapsed time were the same;
   - time-to-completion is itself an OUTCOME, not a nuisance - it should be
     derived and tested;
   - kinetic comparisons need interpolation onto a common basis.

  MONOTONICITY CHECK (residual sugar cannot increase during fermentation):
   74 sample(s) show residual sugar rising by >2 g/L; 63 exceed their own t0 value.
   affected units: 71 of 216; sampling points: ['t1', 't2', 't3']
   worst cases:
     TXL|12|250|R3          t1    sugar  346.49 g/L (+79.9 vs previous; t0 was 266.6; ethanol 0.15%)
     OPA|25|250|R2          t1    sugar  289.29 g/L (+51.5 vs previous; t0 was 237.8; ethanol 0.16%)
     TXL|12|250|R2          t2    sugar  297.72 g/L (+41.9 vs previous; t0 was 266.7; ethanol 0.29%)
     OPA|25|250|R3          t1    sugar  281.51 g/L (+40.0 vs previous; t0 was 241.5; ethanol 0.17%)
     DV10|25|210|R1         t1    sugar  241.47 g/L (+35.4 vs previous; t0 was 206.1; ethanol 0.20%)
   These are analytical artefacts (likely HPLC dilution errors), not
   biology: sugar cannot be produced during fermentation. FLAGGED, not
   deleted - they are excluded only from kinetic fits, and they cannot
   affect endpoint results because all of them occur at early samples.
